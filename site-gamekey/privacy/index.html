Overview

Cerberus Market is a marketplace for buying and selling digital game keys, operated by the Cerberus Market team ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data we collect from buyers and sellers, how it's processed, and what rights you have over it.

The short version: we collect what's needed to run a marketplace — accounts, orders, delivered keys, and seller payout details — and nothing beyond that. We don't sell your data to advertisers, and we don't track you across other sites.

Information we collect from buyers

When you create an account or make a purchase, we collect:

  • Account information -- email address, username, and a securely hashed password (or your identity provider token if you sign in with a third party).
  • Purchase history -- the titles you've bought, order dates, amounts paid, and which seller fulfilled each order.
  • Payment information -- handled by our third-party payment processor. We store a payment method reference (e.g., last 4 digits, card brand, or wallet type) for your convenience, but we never store full card numbers, CVVs, or bank credentials on our own servers.
  • Delivery details -- the email address or account identifier keys are delivered to, and delivery timestamps.
  • Support and dispute records -- messages you send us about missing, invalid, or already-used keys, and the resolution of those disputes.

Information we collect from sellers

If you list keys for sale on Cerberus Market, we additionally collect:

  • Seller verification information -- identity verification documents required for our Verified and Elite seller tiers, and business details if you're selling as an organization.
  • Payout information -- bank account or payout-provider details used to send you proceeds from sales, and tax identification information where required by law.
  • Listing and inventory data -- the titles, regions, and quantities you list, along with pricing history.
  • Performance metrics -- delivery speed, dispute rate, and buyer ratings used to calculate your seller standing and tier eligibility.
Payout account numbers and verification documents are encrypted at rest and are never displayed to buyers or other sellers. Only the information needed to process a transaction is shared between buyer and seller.

Key delivery and order data

Digital keys purchased through Cerberus Market are held in escrow by our platform until an order is confirmed, then delivered to the buyer through our secure delivery system. We log delivery events (time, method, and delivery status) to support refund and replacement claims for invalid or already-used keys. We do not store key contents any longer than necessary to complete delivery and support the buyer's replacement window.

Cookies and local storage

Cerberus Market uses a small number of essential cookies and browser storage mechanisms to make the site work:

  • Session cookie -- keeps you signed in. Expires when you sign out or after a period of inactivity.
  • Local storage (cart state) -- your shopping cart contents are stored in your browser's local storage so your cart persists between visits and page reloads, even before you sign in. This data stays on your device and is only sent to our servers when you check out.
  • Preferences -- display preferences such as region and currency selection, stored locally in your browser.

We do not use third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking pixels. No cookie consent banner is shown because we only use strictly necessary cookies and locally-scoped browser storage.

How data is shared with sellers and processors

To fulfill an order, we share limited information between buyers and sellers:

  • Sellers see the order ID, purchase timestamp, and the delivery address/account you provide for that order -- never your full account email, payment details, or purchase history with other sellers.
  • Buyers see the seller's public storefront name, verification tier, and public rating -- never a seller's payout details or identity verification documents.

We also share data with a small number of service providers strictly to operate the marketplace: our payment processor (to process transactions), our payout provider (to pay sellers), and infrastructure providers who host our servers under standard data processing agreements. None of these providers are permitted to use your data for their own marketing purposes.

Data retention

  • Order and delivery records -- retained for 3 years to support refund windows, dispute resolution, and tax/accounting requirements.
  • Account information -- retained while your account is active, and for a limited period after closure in case of pending disputes.
  • Seller payout and verification records -- retained for as long as required by applicable financial and tax regulations, then securely deleted.
  • Support tickets -- retained for 2 years, then permanently deleted.

After retention periods expire, data is permanently deleted from our systems, including backups on their normal rotation schedule.

Your rights (GDPR, CCPA, and similar frameworks)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access -- request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction -- ask us to correct inaccurate account or profile information.
  • Deletion -- request deletion of your account and associated data, subject to retention obligations described above (for example, completed order records may need to be kept for tax purposes).
  • Portability -- receive your order history and account data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection -- object to certain uses of your data, such as non-essential communications.

To exercise any of these rights, contact privacy@arsenalrx.dev. We respond to all requests within 30 days.

Security

We encrypt data in transit (TLS 1.3) and sensitive data at rest (AES-256), including payment references, payout details, and seller verification documents. Access to buyer and seller data is limited to employees who need it to operate the marketplace, and access is logged. No online system is 100% secure, but we work to keep this one honest about its limits rather than overstate them.

International transfers

Cerberus Market operates across multiple regions, including US-East, EU-West, and AP-Southeast. Depending on your region, your data may be processed in a data center outside your home country. Where we transfer data from the EU to other regions, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) as our legal basis, and all transfers use encrypted channels under the same retention and deletion policies described above.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this policy as Cerberus Market evolves. Material changes will be announced on our changelog and, where required, communicated directly to affected users. The "last updated" date at the top of this page always reflects the most recent revision.
