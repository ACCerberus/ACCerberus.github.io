Every aid to navigation, one tap away.

For Coast Guard Aids-to-Navigation crews: all 54,000 lights and buoys on one map, with the boat ramps, hotels, tides and weather around them. Plan trips and take it on the water, even with no signal.

What it does

Every light, and what's around it.

Every aid

All lights and buoys from the USCG Light List, in every district. Search by name or number.

Ramps and hotels

Boat ramps, marinas and places to stay near each light, with a phone number you can tap.

Water and weather

Tides, river levels, dams, water temperature and the forecast.

Charts

NOAA nautical charts, satellite and street maps, and a tool to measure distance.

Trip planner

Pick the lights; it plans the days, the ramps and where to stay.

Works offline

Save your district before you go. It keeps working with no signal.

Two editions

Use it in the browser, or install it.

Web edition

Runs in your browser, on a computer or a phone. Add it to your home screen and save your district to use it with no signal.

Windows app
