Every aid to navigation, one tap away.

A planning tool for Coast Guard Aids-to-Navigation work: all 54,000 aids of the Light List with the boat ramps, lodging, tides, river gages, dams and weather around them. Runs in your browser, on a phone too, and as a Windows app that plans whole trips and works with no connection.

What it does

One map, one detail card, everything named.

Every aid, every district

Lights, buoys, daybeacons and bridges from the official NAVCEN Light List, refreshed weekly, searchable by name, Light List number or waterway, filtered by district.

Ramps, lodging and parks

Boat ramps, marinas, hotels and parks with a ramp, each properly named with an address and a phone number you can tap. Never a hostel.

Water and weather

Tide and current predictions with charts, USGS gage level, flow and temperature, dams, water temperature at any point and the NWS forecast.

Charts and measurement

NOAA electronic navigational charts as an overlay, street, satellite and topo base maps to street level, and a measure tool with distance and true bearing.

Trip planner desktop

Days split along the waterway, launch and recovery ramps chained, lodging picked last, exported as a PDF work plan.

Works offline desktop

The Windows app caches the Light List, map tiles, imagery, stations and places under AppData, so it runs on the ramp with no signal.

Two editions

Use it in the browser, or install it.

Web edition

Runs entirely in your browser from this site; there is no LightKeeper server. The Light List is rebuilt here every week, places, tides, gages and forecasts come straight from the public services, and everything you look at is cached on your device so the app keeps working offline for areas you have visited.

Windows app

Everything the web edition does, plus the trip planner, USACE dam pool and release data, NOAA chart and satellite tiles downloaded for whole districts, backups and optional routing keys. All data is cached under AppData, so it runs with no connection at all.
