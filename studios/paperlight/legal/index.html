Privacy summary

This website collects nothing. There are no analytics, no advertising or tracking scripts, and no cookies set by us. Webfonts are loaded from a third-party font service, which will see your IP address as part of serving them; nothing else about your visit is recorded here.

Our games collect two kinds of data. Account data — the identifier from your game store, plus anything you choose to give us such as an email address for support — and telemetry, meaning gameplay and crash data used to fix bugs and balance matches. Pocket Kite Club is single-player, requires no account, and sends nothing unless you opt in to crash reporting. Skybreak Royale is an online game and requires an account to play.

Anti-cheat. Skybreak Royale uses a third-party anti-cheat service. It includes a kernel-mode component that starts when the game starts and stops when the game closes; it is not active on your machine at any other time, and we do not receive personal data from it beyond what is needed to enforce the game's rules and to answer an appeal. Our provider's own terms and privacy notice apply to that component and are linked from the game's store page.

We keep account data for as long as your account exists and telemetry for up to eighteen months. We do not sell personal data. If you are in the EU or UK, you can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by writing to support@paperlightgames.com; we will answer within thirty days.
