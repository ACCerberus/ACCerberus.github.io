Games

Everything we have released runs on Windows PC through Steam. Requirements below are the ones we actually test against on our own machines — if your rig sits near the minimum, the game will run, it just will not be pretty.

Skybreak Royale

2023 PC (Steam) Free-to-play

A 60-player battle royale where every player keeps a glider on their back, so the map is as much vertical as it is flat. Matches run about twelve minutes, the art is bright cut-paper rather than mud and rubble, and seasons add an island or a traversal change rather than a new storefront.

Built in Unity. Live in three regions — US-East, EU-West and AP-Southeast — with seasonal updates roughly every ten weeks.

Minimum

Pocket Kite Club

2021 PC (Steam) Single-player

Our first release, and still the one people send us letters about. You inherit a shed full of half-finished kites on a small island and spend an afternoon — or forty hours — flying them, mending them and finding out where the wind goes. There is no failure state and no timer.

Built in Unity. Single-player and fully offline; no account required.
