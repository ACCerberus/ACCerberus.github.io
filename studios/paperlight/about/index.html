Founded 2019 · Lisbon, Portugal

About the studio

Paperlight started as two people at a game jam in Lisbon with a kite prototype and a scanner full of torn coloured paper. The jam build turned into Pocket Kite Club, which sold well enough to pay two salaries for a year; a small regional arts grant covered the second year while we figured out what a multiplayer version of the same feeling would be. That became Skybreak Royale.

We are twenty-nine people now, and we intend to stay roughly that size. Growing past thirty would mean taking outside money, and outside money would mean shipping something every year. We would rather keep two games healthy and go home at six.
