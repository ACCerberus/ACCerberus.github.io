The studio

Small enough to argue, big enough to ship

Molten Anvil Interactive started in 2014 as a group of contractors in Kraków who got tired of building other people's games. Two shipped titles later we are still independent, still self-funded, and still spending most of our week on the unglamorous parts — netcode, hit registration, live operations and the patch after the patch.

How we work

01

Iterate in the build

Design documents lose to playable builds. Every feature goes into a playtest within a sprint, and most of them die there. The ones that survive get rebuilt at least once.

02

Hitreg is the product

Competitive shooters live or die on whether a shot feels honest. We instrument rollback, tick rate and server reconciliation continuously and treat a regression there as a stop-ship bug.

03

Ship, then keep shipping

Launch is the first week of a long job. Live operations sit with the team that built the systems, not in a separate room, so fixes land in days rather than quarters.

Flagship title

Five-versus-five attack and defend, fought over objectives inside buildings that come apart as you fight through them. Destructible cover means no angle stays safe for a whole round, and every wall you break is a wall the other squad can use next.
