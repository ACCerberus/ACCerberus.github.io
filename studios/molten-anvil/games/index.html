Games

Two titles, both on PC, both still receiving updates. Requirements below are measured on our own test bench at 1080p unless noted.

Ironclad Siege

Squad-based competitive shooter
Released 2022 PC (Steam) Unreal Engine 5 5v5 attack / defend

Two squads of five trade attack and defend rounds over fixed objectives inside industrial interiors that break apart as the round runs. Cover is destructible down to the structural elements, so a sightline that was safe in round one is a hole by round six.

Ironclad Siege runs a dedicated-server authoritative model with client-side prediction and server reconciliation. It has been in continuous live operation since launch, with a ranked season roughly every ten weeks.

Engine note: built on Unreal Engine 5, with an in-house destruction and replication layer sitting on top of the standard networking stack. The game moved from Unreal Engine 4 to 5 during the 2023 off-season.

Anti-cheat: Cerberus (kernel-mode; loads when the game launches, unloads when it exits) — currently in closed beta. It is not installed as a permanently resident service, and it is not required to play Anvilborn.

Anvilborn

Co-op survival defence
Released 2017 PC (Steam) Unity 1–4 players co-op

Our first release. Up to four players hold a fortified position through escalating night assaults, spending the daylight hours repairing walls, moving supplies and deciding which approach to give up. It very nearly ended the studio and then quietly kept it alive.

Anvilborn is feature-complete but not abandoned. We still ship compatibility fixes, occasional balance passes and community-requested quality-of-life changes when the schedule allows.

Engine note: built in Unity, with peer-hosted co-op sessions rather than dedicated servers.
