The studio

About

Founded 2016. Portland, Oregon. Thirty-eight people, most of whom came from somewhere other than games.

Origin

Vantage Point Games came out of a milsim mod scene. Ruth Halvorsen and Tomás Berger had been maintaining a large ballistics and squad-command mod for several years, mostly at night, and by 2015 it had a bug tracker, a release cadence, and a small group of people who treated it like a job. In 2016 they registered a company in Portland so those people could be paid.

We were self-funded until 2019, which meant contract work paid for the parts of Longshadow Company that the mod could not. The campaign shipped in November 2019 and did well enough that we stopped taking contracts. Ashfall Protocol followed in 2024 and is what most of the studio works on now.

We have grown slowly and on purpose. Thirty-eight people is roughly the size at which everyone still knows what everyone else is doing, and we would rather stay near it than not.
