What we do not accept

We do not accept unsolicited pitches, game concepts, design documents or code. Anything of that sort sent to us is deleted unread, because reading it creates problems for both of us later.

We also do not take outsourcing, co-development, marketing agency or asset-store approaches. We came out of the outsourcing business ourselves and we know how many of these emails a studio our size gets in a week — please do not add to it.
