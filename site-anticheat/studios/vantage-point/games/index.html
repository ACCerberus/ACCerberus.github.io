Games

Two titles, both PC-only, both on Steam. Requirements below are measured on a 1080p target at 60 frames per second unless stated otherwise.

Ashfall Protocol

2024 · PC (Steam)

Tactical military shooter · 12v12 objective · Ranked ladder · Unreal Engine 5

Two squads of twelve contest a fixed objective across open, long-sightline terrain. Attack and defence swap at the half, and the ranked ladder runs in seasons with a partial reset each time. Weapon handling is data-driven and fully exposed in the loadout screen, so a player can check the numbers before they commit to a build.

Ashfall Protocol is in continuous live operations. A content season lands roughly every four months and brings a map, a weapon pass, and a ranked reset.

Longshadow Company

2019 · PC (Steam)

Squad-level tactical campaign · Single-player and four-player co-op · Unreal Engine 4

The studio’s first release. A twelve-mission campaign in which you command a four-soldier element through planning, movement, and contact, with orders given from a map view and executed in real time. It plays alone or with up to three others, each taking a soldier.

Longshadow Company is feature-complete and no longer receives content updates. We still fix crashes and compatibility issues as they are reported.
