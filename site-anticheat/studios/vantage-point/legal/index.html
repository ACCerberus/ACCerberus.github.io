Privacy summary

This website

This site collects nothing. There are no analytics, no advertising tags, no cookies, and no tracking scripts. We do not set any browser storage. Web fonts are loaded from a third-party font service, which will see your IP address as part of serving those files; nothing else about your visit is shared. Our host keeps standard server access logs for a short period for security and diagnostic purposes.

Our games

Our games collect account data and gameplay telemetry. Account data is the identifier supplied by the platform you bought the game on, plus any contact address you give us. Telemetry covers match results, ranked rating, crash reports, and performance samples, and is used to run the ladder, fix defects, and tune balance. We do not sell it and we do not share it for advertising.

Ashfall Protocol’s anti-cheat is provided by a third party and runs only while the game is running; it starts with the game and stops when the game exits.

To request a copy of your data or its deletion, email support@vantagepointgames.com from the address on the account. Deleting an account removes the ability to play a purchased title.
