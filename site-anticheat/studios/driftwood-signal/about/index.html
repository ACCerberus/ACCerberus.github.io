About

A small studio on a cold coast.

Founded in 2020. Twenty-three people. Two games shipped, and no particular ambition to be larger than this.

How we got here

Driftwood Signal started in 2020 as five people working remotely on a survival game nobody had asked for. When it became clear the project was real, most of us moved east and put an office in Halifax, because the rent made sense and the weather suited what we were making.

We self-published Saltline in 2021. It did modestly well and taught us how to finish. Undercroft Tactics followed in 2024 and is what most of the studio works on now.

We have turned down growth more than once. Past roughly twenty-five people, we would need a layer of management we do not want and could not honestly pay for through a quiet year. Staying small is a choice we re-make every time we open a role.
