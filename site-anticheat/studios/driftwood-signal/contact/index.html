A note on pitches

We do not accept unsolicited pitches, design documents, concept art or game ideas, and we delete them unread. This protects both of us: we work on a small number of things for a long time, and we cannot risk an accidental overlap with something already in progress.

If you want to work with us, the honest route is an open role.

Elsewhere

We are @driftwoodsignal on most platforms. We do not link out to them from here, and we will never ask you for account details, payment information or a download link over a direct message.

Anything that matters — patch notes, wipe dates, downtime — is published on our own channels first.
