Games about what it costs to come back.

We make quiet, tense, high-stakes games. Twenty-three people on the North Atlantic coast, working slowly and on purpose.

The studio

Driftwood Signal was founded in 2020 and has shipped two games. We work on long timelines, keep the team small, and try to build things that hold their tension after the novelty wears off.

Our players tend to stay a long time. That shapes almost everything we do — how we patch, how we handle loss, how seriously we take the health of a match.

What we’re chasing

  • Weight

    A decision should be felt before it is understood. We would rather a player hesitate at a door than clear it perfectly.

  • Consequence

    Loss is permanent where we say it is. Systems that let you undo a mistake quietly take the meaning out of avoiding one.

  • Fair ground

    A small competitive scene only works if the match is honest. We spend real engineering time on that, and we say what we are doing.
