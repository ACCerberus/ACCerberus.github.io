Games

Two games, six years apart.

Both on PC, both on Steam. Requirements below are what we actually test against, not what we hope you have.

Undercroft Tactics

2024 · PC (Steam)

A tactical extraction shooter set beneath a flooded industrial coast. You choose a loadout, drop into a raid with a handful of other players, and try to leave with more than you came in with.

Death is permanent for everything you were carrying. There is no insurance on a bad decision, and the walk back to an extraction point is usually the hardest part of the run.

Built in Unreal Engine 5. Ongoing development, with a published wipe schedule and a small, tight-knit competitive scene we know most of by name.

Minimum
OSWindows 10 21H2 or later / Windows 11 (64-bit)
CPUQuad-core, 3.4 GHz (Intel Core i5-8400 / AMD Ryzen 5 2600)
RAM12 GB
GPU6 GB VRAM, DirectX 12 (GTX 1660 / RX 5600 XT)
Storage62 GB, SSD required
Anti-cheat: Cerberus (kernel-mode; loads when the game launches, unloads when it exits)
Recommended
OSWindows 10 21H2 or later / Windows 11 (64-bit)
CPUSix-core, 3.7 GHz (Intel Core i5-12600K / AMD Ryzen 5 5600X)
RAM16 GB
GPU8 GB VRAM, DirectX 12 (RTX 3060 Ti / RX 6700 XT)
Storage62 GB, NVMe SSD
Anti-cheat: Cerberus (kernel-mode; loads when the game launches, unloads when it exits)

Undercroft Tactics requires Secure Boot and TPM 2.0 to be enabled. If you would rather not run kernel-mode anti-cheat, we would rather you did not buy the game than be surprised by it after the fact.

Saltline

2021 · PC (Steam)

Our first release. A single-player coastal survival and exploration game about walking a long shore alone, keeping a light burning, and reading what the tide leaves behind.

No combat to speak of, no timers pushing you along. It is a slow game, and it was made by a slow team learning how to finish something.

Built in Unreal Engine 4. Complete and no longer in active development; we still patch it when something breaks.

Minimum
OSWindows 10 (64-bit)
CPUDual-core, 3.0 GHz (Intel Core i3-6100 / AMD FX-6300)
RAM8 GB
GPU3 GB VRAM, DirectX 11 (GTX 1050 Ti / RX 560)
Storage24 GB
Recommended
OSWindows 10 (64-bit)
CPUQuad-core, 3.4 GHz (Intel Core i5-8400 / AMD Ryzen 5 2600)
RAM12 GB
GPU6 GB VRAM, DirectX 11 (GTX 1660 / RX 5600 XT)
Storage24 GB, SSD

Saltline is single-player and offline-capable. It ships with no anti-cheat.
