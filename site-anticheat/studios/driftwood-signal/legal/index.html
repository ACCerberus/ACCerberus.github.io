Privacy summary

This website. It collects nothing. There are no analytics, no advertising tags, no cookies and no third-party scripts. Our host keeps standard access logs, including IP addresses, for a short period for security and abuse purposes. Web fonts are loaded from a third-party font service, which will see your IP address as part of that request.

Our games. Saltline is single-player and sends us nothing. Undercroft Tactics requires an account and collects what an online game needs: your account identifier, purchase and entitlement records, match and progression data, crash reports and gameplay telemetry used for balance, stability and cheat detection.

Anti-cheat. Undercroft Tactics’ anti-cheat is provided by a third party under contract to us. It runs only while the game is running, starts when the game launches and stops when the game exits, and it processes hashed device and platform-integrity signals rather than personal information about you. It does not run in the background when you are not playing.

We do not sell personal data. We retain account data while your account exists and for a limited period afterwards for fraud, refund and enforcement purposes. To request a copy of your data or its deletion, email support@driftwoodsignal.com from the address on the account.
