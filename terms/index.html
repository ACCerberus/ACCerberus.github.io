Refunds and replacement policy

If a key doesn't work, we fix it. Report an invalid or already-used key within 48 hours of purchase and we'll issue a free replacement or a full refund -- your choice.
  • Invalid keys -- if a key fails to activate, submit a support ticket with the error you received. We verify the claim with the seller and issue a replacement key or refund, typically within 24-48 hours.
  • Already-used keys -- if a key was already redeemed by someone else, this is treated the same as an invalid key and is eligible for immediate replacement or refund.
  • Buyer's remorse -- once a key has been revealed to you, it cannot be returned for a refund, since keys cannot be "un-redeemed." We recommend reviewing region and platform requirements before purchase.
  • Delivery delays -- if a manually-fulfilled order isn't delivered within 24 hours, you may cancel for a full refund.

Refunds are returned to your original payment method or as Cerberus Market wallet balance, at your choice, where available.

Seller terms

To list keys for sale on Cerberus Market:

  • Payout schedule -- proceeds from completed sales (net of the marketplace fee) are paid out weekly to your registered payout method, after the buyer's replacement window has closed.

Dispute resolution

Most order issues are resolved automatically through our refund and replacement policy above. For disputes that aren't resolved that way:

  1. Open a dispute through your order page, describing the issue and any evidence (error screenshots, activation logs).
  2. The counterparty (buyer or seller) has 3 business days to respond.
  3. If unresolved, our support team reviews the evidence and makes a binding determination on the order -- typically within 5 business days.
  4. Funds in dispute are held in escrow until resolution, so neither party is at risk of losing both the item and the payment.

Repeated disputes against a single seller may trigger a broader account review independent of any individual case.
